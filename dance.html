Our Story

The couple’s journey together began on the campus of UCSB in a ballroom dance class, where Laetitia met Porfirio, the teaching assistant. This initial connection quickly blossomed into a shared competitive ambition: to reach the pinnacle of ballroom dance. They dedicated themselves to the demanding American Style—Waltz, Tango, Foxtrot, and Viennese Waltz—seeking out world-class coaches to hone their craft.

Despite maintaining professional careers in the fast-paced tech industry as product marketers, Porfirio and Laetitia became a dominant force on the competitive circuit. They achieved four consecutive appearances in the National Final (Top 6) at the USA Dance Nationals, culminating in a National Silver Medal in 2005. Driven by the desire for greater challenge and opportunity, the pair transitioned to the Professional category, which offered a deeper field of competitors and the chance to earn prize purses.

Having achieved their goals as competitors, their focus shifted to educating the next generation of dancers. They partnered with their coaches to create widely successful on-line instructional resources: The Quick Start Guide to Ballroom Dancing (covering Waltz, Tango, Salsa, and Swing) and the specialized Forget the Feet Wedding Dance Program. Although retired from competitive life, they remain passionate about dance and are available for private bookings, consulting, and choreography for stage, film, and television.
