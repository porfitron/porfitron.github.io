1990 Ford F-250 XLT Lariat SuperCab 4x4

7.5L 460 EFI · Banks PowerPack · E4OD Automatic

Private Listing

Overview

This 1990 Ford F-250 XLT Lariat SuperCab 4x4 has spent its entire life in California. VIN 1FTHX26G5LKA11678 was built at Ford’s Kansas City plant as an F-250 HD SuperCab 4x4 with the 7.5-liter 460 EFI V8. It is paired with an E4OD four-speed automatic, a dual-range transfer case, and manually locking front hubs. Dual fuel tanks are fitted, and the in-cab selector switch works.

A Gale Banks PowerPack is installed; the Banks badge remains on the left front fender. The truck was used to support CAL FIRE while driven by a private contractor, a retired Fire Captain, then as a city work truck for the owner’s property-management business—hauling supplies and making dump runs. It is offered from Paso Robles.

Engine and Drivetrain

The 7.5-liter 460 is factory-rated at 230 horsepower and 390 lb-ft. The Banks PowerPack on this truck is the gasoline breathing system: Ram-Air intake, TorqueTube exhaust manifolds, and a free-flowing exhaust. Power goes to the rear or all four wheels through the E4OD and a dual-range transfer case. Front axle is a Dana 50 Twin-Traction Beam.

Body and Interior

SuperCab, eight-foot bed, chrome towing mirrors, running boards with amber marker lamps, sliding rear window, and aftermarket eight-lug wheels on all-terrain tires. The truck was painted white shortly after the Fire Captain bought it, mainly to cover clear-coat and sun damage. Interior is Scarlet Red cloth.

The bed was cut for a fifth-wheel hitch, which has since been removed. The floor is currently covered with a sheet of plywood.
