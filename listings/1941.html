1941 Ford Cabriolet

Street Rod · Black over Cream Leather

Private Listing

Overview

This 1941 Ford Cabriolet is a black street rod trimmed in cream leather, offered from Paso Robles. The body is finished in a deep gloss black that mirrors the driveway and the trees around it. A black canvas convertible top is shown both raised and folded, and California plates are fitted.

Brightwork runs the length of the car: a tall vertical-bar chrome grille, chrome headlight rings and mirrors, a chrome side spear, chrome window moldings, and chrome bumpers front and rear. Whitewall tires wear chrome hubcaps. Dual exhaust tips exit beneath the rear bumper.

Body and Exterior

The two-door cabriolet sits low over the whitewalls, with chrome running boards along the rockers and a single bright spear tracing the beltline. Sealed-beam headlights sit in the fenders beside the vertical grille. At the rear, small vertical taillamps flank a chrome bumper with a Ford script, and the deck lid carries a smooth black finish with the top boot in place when the canvas is folded.

With the top down, cream headrests rise above the seat and the folded canvas stays behind the cabin. With the top up, the black canvas and its rear window close the silhouette.

Interior

The cabin is upholstered in cream leather with beige inserts and piping. The bench, door panels, and rear cushion share the same trim. Chrome window cranks and interior handles sit in the door panels, and lap belts cross the seat. A banjo-style steering wheel with chrome spokes faces a set of round gauges.
