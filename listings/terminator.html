2003 Toyota Tundra "Terminator 3" Special Edition

~1 of 800 Built to Commemorate Rise of the Machines

Private Listing

This 2003 Toyota Tundra Access Cab is one of a limited number of "Terminator 3" Special Edition models produced to commemorate the truck's feature in Terminator 3: Rise of the Machines. The 4-door Access Cab configuration features a rear bench seat, adding valuable interior spaciousness and practicality. Total production was limited to approximately 800 units, making it a rare survivor among first-generation Tundras. This monochromatic black example is a two-owner California truck that has been garaged since new and meticulously maintained. Originally purchased by the current owner's stepfather, this vehicle boasts a continuous family history and a lifetime of documented care.

T3 Edition & Factory Options

The Terminator 3 Special Edition Package equips the Tundra with a unique list of factory performance and aesthetic upgrades. As detailed on the window sticker, this includes a unique TRD dual-exit exhaust system, dark gray perforated leather seats, and 17-inch titanium-toned alloy wheels. The chassis is upgraded with a Sport Suspension featuring Tokico shocks. Exterior distinctions include a custom T3 grille with logo, full color-keyed exterior cladding, T3 badging, and fog lamps. Notably, the T3 package features a factory "de-badged" look, omitting standard Tundra emblems for a cleaner, stealthier aesthetic. Inside, the cabin is finished with unique metallic-appearance interior trim.

In addition to the T3 package, this truck was optioned with the factory Towing Package. This includes a Class IV towing receiver hitch, a 7-pin connector, a heavy-duty 130-amp alternator, a trailer brake controller, and a supplemental transmission oil cooler, ensuring the truck is ready for hauling duty.

To ensure the utility of the truck was never compromised, the original owner promptly installed a color-matched SnugTop fiberglass Tonneau cover and a fitted BedRug. These additions have perfectly preserved the bed surface since new and are included in the sale.

Interior and Tech Upgrades

The cabin features the exclusive T3 dark leather upholstery, which is in exceptional condition with no tears or significant wear. Recent modernization includes an Android Auto and Apple CarPlay compatible touchscreen head unit and a tailgate handle-mounted backup camera. While the HVAC knobs and audio system have been updated for modern use, the original OEM stereo, HVAC knobs, and factory tailgate handle have all been saved and are included. The door keyholes have been deleted for a sleek aesthetic, and the truck now features a modern keyless entry system, WeatherTech floor mats, and an interior LED lighting upgrade.

Condition and Recent Maintenance

Showing approximately 83,000 miles, this Tundra has been preserved as a collector-grade example. Maintenance has included regular synthetic oil changes, the completion of all factory recalls, and the truck recently passed California Emissions testing with no issues. In August 2025, a fresh set of Goodyear Workhorse A/T tires was installed. As a California vehicle, the chassis remains remarkably clean with no rust. The truck has been garaged throughout its life, preserving the depth of the monochromatic black finish. To protect the front-end, a custom full leather bra was commissioned by the first owner; it has never been used and is included with the sale. The 4.7L i-Force V8 remains strong, and the current owner has acquired a full set of replacement/back-up T3 bed decals to ensure the truck's unique branding can be maintained for years to come.
