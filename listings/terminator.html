2003 Toyota Tundra SR5 "Terminator 3" Special Edition

~1 of 800 Built to Commemorate Rise of the Machines

Private Listing

This 2003 Toyota Tundra SR5 is one of a limited number of "Terminator 3" Special Edition models produced to commemorate the truck's feature in Terminator 3: Rise of the Machines. Total production was limited to approximately 800 units, making it a rare survivor among first-generation Tundras. This monochromatic black example is a two-owner California truck that has been garaged since new and meticulously maintained. Originally purchased by the seller's stepfather, this vehicle boasts a continuous family history and a lifetime of documented care.

T3 Edition Features

The Terminator 3 package transformed the Tundra into a street-performance icon. Key factory upgrades include a T3-specific grille, monochromatic black paint, unique 17-inch alloy wheels, and specialized T3 badging. Performance is enhanced by a factory TRD dual exhaust system. The exterior has been protected since day one by a custom full leather bra commissioned by the first owner, which remains included in the sale. Additionally, the bed is in impeccable condition thanks to a fiberglass Tonneau cover and Bedrug installed upon delivery.

Interior and Tech Upgrades

The cabin features the exclusive T3 dark leather upholstery, which is in exceptional condition with no tears or significant wear. Recent modernization includes an Android Auto and Apple CarPlay compatible touchscreen head unit and a tailgate handle-mounted backup camera. While the HVAC knobs and audio system have been updated for modern use, the original OEM stereo, HVAC knobs, and factory tailgate handle have all been saved and are included. The door keyholes have been deleted for a sleek aesthetic, and the truck now features a modern keyless entry system, WeatherTech floor mats, and an interior LED lighting upgrade.

Condition and Recent Maintenance

Showing approximately 83,000 miles, this Tundra has been preserved as a collector-grade example. In August 2025, a fresh set of Goodyear Workhorse A/T tires was installed. As a California vehicle, the chassis remains remarkably clean with no rust. The truck has been garaged throughout its life, preserving the depth of the monochromatic black finish. The 4.7L i-Force V8 remains strong, and the seller has acquired a full set of replacement/back-up T3 bed decals to ensure the truck's unique branding can be maintained for years to come.
