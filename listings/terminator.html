The Terminator 3 Special Edition

In the summer of 2003, Toyota partnered with the release of the blockbuster action film Terminator 3: Rise of the Machines to create a truck with a cinematic presence. The result was the Tundra "T3" Special Edition, a limited-run package designed to mirror the tough, high-tech persona of Arnold Schwarzenegger's iconic character. With only approximately 850 units produced (roughly 650 for the U.S. and 200 for Canada), the T3 Edition transformed the SR5 Access Cab into a menacing street-performance machine.

The factory enhancements were extensive and purpose-built for visual aggression and improved driving dynamics. The exterior was treated to a full monochromatic black-out package, including a color-keyed grille, bumpers, and trim, while removing standard badging for a stealthier look. It rolled on unique 17-inch titanium-toned alloy wheels and announced its presence with a custom TRD dual-exit exhaust system that delivered a deeper, more authoritative exhaust note. Underneath, a sport-tuned suspension with Tokico shocks and a heavy-duty stabilizer bar sharpened the truck's handling, making it feel more planted than a standard pickup. Inside, the cabin received unique dark gray perforated leather seats and metallic-appearance trim, solidifying its status as a true collector's piece.

Vehicle Specifics & Upgrades

This particular example is a two-owner California truck with a rich family history, having been originally purchased by the current owner's stepfather. It is an Access Cab model, offering the convenience of four doors and a spacious rear bench seat. In addition to the standard T3 equipment, this truck was optioned with the factory Towing Package, which adds a Class IV receiver hitch, 7-pin connector, heavy-duty 130-amp alternator, trailer brake controller, and supplemental transmission cooler.

Preservation and modernization have been key priorities for this vehicle. The original owner commissioned a custom full leather front bra—which has remained unused and is included—and installed a SnugTop fiberglass tonneau cover and fitted BedRug immediately after purchase, keeping the bed in pristine condition. The current owner has updated the technology for daily usability, adding a touchscreen head unit with Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, along with a tailgate handle-mounted backup camera. For a cleaner aesthetic, the exterior door keyholes were deleted in favor of a modern keyless entry system. The interior is further protected by WeatherTech floor mats and enhanced with a full LED lighting upgrade. Crucially, all original parts replaced during these upgrades—including the OEM stereo, HVAC knobs, and tailgate handle—have been retained and are included in the sale.

Condition & Maintenance

With approximately 83,000 miles on the odometer, this T3 Tundra presents as a well-cared-for survivor. It has lived its entire life in California, ensuring a chassis free of the rust issues common in other regions. The truck has been consistently garaged, preserving the depth and gloss of the unique monochromatic black paint. Mechanically, it is powered by the legendary 4.7L i-Force V8, known for its million-mile durability. Maintenance has been rigorous, including regular synthetic oil changes and the resolution of all factory recalls. The vehicle recently passed California emissions testing with no issues. In August 2025, a new set of Goodyear Workhorse A/T tires was installed, ensuring it is ready for the road. The sale also includes a full set of replacement T3 bed decals acquired by the owner, ensuring the truck's unique identity can be maintained indefinitely.
