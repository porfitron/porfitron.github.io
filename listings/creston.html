About this community

Private, clean, and quiet eight-unit townhome community in Paso Robles. Homes were recently refreshed with new flooring and paint so they show well and are easy to maintain.

Each layout is about 1,000 sq. ft.: living room, dining area, kitchen, and half bath on the ground floor; two bedrooms and a full bath upstairs—practical for roommates or a small household.

Your own fenced yard with a patio and low-maintenance artificial turf—room to unwind without a big landscaping bill. The neighborhood is handy to day-to-day needs: groceries, schools, wine country, and Highway 101 for commutes elsewhere in San Luis Obispo County.
