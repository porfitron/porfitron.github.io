1934 Ford Model 40 Deluxe Cabriolet Hot Rod

Custom Build by Scotts Hot Rod Shop

Private Listing

Overview

This 1934 Ford Model 40 Deluxe Cabriolet is a custom-built hot rod that was fabricated by Scotts Hot Rod Shop, formerly of Ventura, California. Finished in Vermillion Red over gray leather upholstery, the car is powered by a ZZ4 350ci V8 paired with a 700-R4 four-speed automatic transmission and a Currie Positraction 9-inch rear end.

Body and Exterior

The steel body is finished in Vermillion Red and features a black canvas soft top with a powder-coated frame and a matching boot cover. The paint displays a deep, mirror-like finish consistent with its "Show Quality" designation in recent appraisals. Brightwork includes chrome front and rear bumpers, a chrome grille, 9" headlights, dual chrome horns, and chrome cowl lights. The car is equipped with suicide doors, rubber-tread running boards, a tinted windshield, and chrome side mirrors. Painted steel wheels feature chrome hubcaps and trim rings and are currently mounted with Coker classic whitewall tires.

Interior

The cabin features a bench seat upholstered in gray leather with matching door panels and gray closed-loop wool carpeting. The car is equipped with a functional rumble seat also trimmed in gray leather. Comfort is managed by a Vintage Air climate control system providing both air conditioning and heat, along with power windows and cruise control. The dashboard houses Classic Instruments antique-face gauges, including a 120-mph speedometer and a six-digit mechanical odometer showing approximately 4,466 miles.

Mechanicals

The ZZ4 350ci V8 engine is equipped with high-performance cylinder heads and an Edelbrock four-barrel carburetor. The engine bay is highly detailed and features a polished intake manifold, valve covers, air cleaner, alternator, and A/C compressor. Cooling is handled by a polished aluminum radiator with dual electric fans, polished water pipes, and a billet overflow reservoir. Power is sent to the rear wheels through a 700-R4 four-speed automatic transmission and a Ford 9-inch rear end with Currie Positraction gears.
