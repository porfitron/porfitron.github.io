1953 Ford Customline Club Coupe

Ford 50th Anniversary Year · Mainly Original

Private Listing

Overview

This 1953 Ford Customline Club Coupe is a low-mileage, mainly original example from Ford’s 50th anniversary year. Finished in blue over a grey interior, it is powered by the 215-cubic-inch overhead-valve inline-six—the second year of Ford’s OHV six—paired with a column-shifted three-speed manual and overdrive. Approximately 14,000 miles are shown. The car is in good condition and wears California Historical Vehicle plates.

Body and Exterior

The two-door Club Coupe body (factory style 76B) is finished in blue and retains the Customline chrome side spear, rear-quarter hash marks, and bright grille and bumper work. Steel wheels wear hubcaps and whitewall tires. The trunk lid carries Ford’s 50th anniversary crest and the Overdrive script. Period family photographs of the car are included with the listing photos.

Interior

The cabin is trimmed in grey, with a bench seat in grey cloth and pinstripe inserts, matching door panels, and a black rubber floor covering. A three-spoke steering wheel with the anniversary Ford crest sits ahead of the original instrument cluster; the three-speed shifter is on the column, as built. The driver’s door data plate lists serial number A3RS106846.

Mechanicals

Under the hood is Ford’s 215-cubic-inch Mileage Maker overhead-valve inline-six, introduced the year before and fed by a single carburetor. Power goes to the rear wheels through a three-speed manual with overdrive. A spare engine in parts is available separately for $250.
