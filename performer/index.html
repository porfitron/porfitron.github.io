About Me

I am a championship ballroom dancer and performer in San Francisco. With my partner, Laetitia Santore, I competed in American Smooth — Waltz, Tango, Foxtrot, and Viennese Waltz — and reached the USA Dance National Final four years running, including a National Silver Medal in 2005.

That work moved from the competition floor to the stage and onto television and film, including Fox Searchlight’s Antwone Fisher, 20th Century Fox Television’s Point Pleasant, the San Diego Symphony, and Santa Barbara Spanish Days. I also teach through The Quick Start Guide to Ballroom Dancing and the Forget the Feet Wedding Dance Program.

I am retired from competition and available for performance, choreography, and consulting for stage, film, and television.
