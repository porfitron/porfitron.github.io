Ready when you are

Enable sound and keep the screen awake for uninterrupted training.

Silent switch (iPhone)

If the side switch shows orange, sounds stay muted. Flip it off before you begin.

Sound placement

Consider pairing a Bluetooth speaker to your phone, so that the sound comes from another part of the house.

Treats & cues

Give different treats/rewards for clicker vs. whistle. The whistle is for recall, so give them their favorite treat.
